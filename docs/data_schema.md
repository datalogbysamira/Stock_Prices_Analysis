| Column Name | Data Type | Explanation                                                                      |
| ----------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `symbol`    | `string`  | Unique stock ticker symbol identifying the company, e.g., `AAPL`, `AAL`, `MSFT`. |
| `date`      | `date`    | Trading date for the stock price data.                                           |
| `open`      | `float`   | Stock price at the beginning of the trading session.                             |
| `high`      | `float`   | Highest stock price reached during the trading session.                          |
| `low`       | `float`   | Lowest stock price reached during the trading session.                           |
| `close`     | `float`   | Stock price at the end of the trading session.                                   |
| `volume`    | `integer` | Total number of shares traded during the trading session.                        |
